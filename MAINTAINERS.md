## Responsibilities

Maintainers review and merge pull requests, triage issues, and cut releases.
Contributor-facing workflows are documented in
[CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) and
[docs/docs/Contributing/contributing.md](docs/docs/Contributing/contributing.md);
development setup, dependency rules, tests, and code style are in
[docs/docs/Contributing/development.md](docs/docs/Contributing/development.md).

## Becoming a maintainer

Consistent, high-quality contributions (code, reviews, triage) are the path to
maintainership. Current maintainers nominate and approve new maintainers.
